# Sources

Each code is used in the question bank. Use the one-line idea when you must explain why a question matters. Do not quote a source unless the quote is below.

| Code | Source | Main idea for this skill |
|---|---|---|
| GIL | Elad Gil, *High Growth Handbook*, ch. 7 Product management — https://growth.eladgil.com/book/chapter-7-product-management/product-management-overview/ | If you cannot answer the strategy questions (customer, metric, price, channel, what it does *not* do), you are not ready to build. |
| CAGAN | Marty Cagan, *Inspired*; SVPG "The Four Big Risks", "Assessing Product Opportunities" — https://www.svpg.com/four-big-risks/ | Four risks: value, usability, feasibility, viability. Ten-question opportunity assessment ending in go / no-go. |
| DSS | Bob Moesta, *Demand-Side Sales 101* — https://therewiredgroup.com/learn/demand-side-sales-101/ | "If there's no struggle, there's no demand." Four forces, buying timeline, causation not demographics. |
| CHRISTENSEN | Christensen et al., *Competing Against Luck* — https://www.christenseninstitute.org/theory/jobs-to-be-done/ | A job is the progress a person tries to make in a circumstance. Functional, social, emotional. What gets fired? |
| RIES | Eric Ries, *The Lean Startup* — https://theleanstartup.com/principles | Leap-of-faith assumptions, MVP = most learning for least effort, actionable vs vanity metrics, pivot or persevere. |
| KARRI | Lenny's Podcast, Karri Saarinen (Linear) — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4muxFVZ4XfM | Fit per segment, opinionated product for "someone", ship rough early, design is not the reason a company succeeds. |
| SHREYAS | Lenny's Podcast, Shreyas Doshi — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YP_QghPLG-8 | Pre-mortem (tigers, paper tigers, elephants), opportunity cost over ROI, LNO, impact vs execution vs optics. |
| MOESTA | Lenny's Podcast, Bob Moesta (JTBD) — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xQV7HVyAJjc | "A struggling moment causes demand." Interview switchers. "Bitchin' ain't switchin'." Choose what to suck at. |
| WEINSTEIN | Lenny's Podcast, Jeff Weinstein (Stripe) — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qbZQjprTnrU | The outage test, "ready to pay is different than paying", friends count as zero, the "Sarah" level of specificity. |
| HELMER | Lenny's Podcast, Hamilton Helmer (7 Powers) — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKq1_KPSqy0 | Power = benefit + barrier. Counter-positioning first. Castle or shack. Execution is a treadmill, not power. |
| MCALLISTER | Lenny's Podcast, Ian McAllister (Amazon) — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7gaPPrAd7nc | Working backwards: no problem paragraph, no problem. "Ingredients in the pantry." Big idea, right owner, legitimate plan. |
| COLLISON | Invest Like the Best, Patrick & John Collison — https://joincolossus.com/episode/collison-a-business-state-of-mind/ | Frame the market by outcome, power-law customers (one complaint can matter), the "billion dollars to copy you" test. |
| RACHLEFF | Invest Like the Best, Andy Rachleff — https://joincolossus.com/episode/rachleff-building-something-people-want-to-buy/ | PMF = people pull it out of your hands; organic exponential growth. Value hypothesis before growth. Iterate on the who. Right and non-consensus. |
| EK | Invest Like the Best, Daniel Ek — https://joincolossus.com/episode/ek-the-future-of-audio/ | Niche or scale, the middle is vaporized. Define the market by the job. The wedge. Do not copy a mechanic without its system. |
| KRIEGER | Invest Like the Best, Kevin Systrom & Mike Krieger — https://joincolossus.com/episode/krieger-how-to-build-a-great-product/ | Name the top problems and crush them. Build on existing behavior. Technology in search of a problem is "paddling too early". |
| GURLEY | Invest Like the Best, Bill Gurley — https://joincolossus.com/episode/gurley-all-things-business-and-investing/ | Test network effects (value vs penetration). Liquidity in a small area first. Paid growth with no organic tip is lead gen. |
| SHOWRUN | Javier Grillo-Marxuach, *The Eleven Laws of Showrunning* — https://okbjgm.weebly.com/uploads/3/1/5/0/31506003/11_laws_of_showrunning_nice_version.pdf | Know your show and say it. "I'll know it when I see it" is abdication. Avoid the "sexy glamorous jobs". Bad news early. |
| SINGER | Ryan Singer, "Products Are Functions" — https://ryansinger.co/functions/ | Product = f(x) = y. x and y are the requirements; compare with the status quo, not with an ideal. |
| VOHRA | Rahul Vohra, "How Superhuman built an engine to find PMF" — https://review.firstround.com/how-superhuman-built-an-engine-to-find-product-market-fit/ | ≥40% "very disappointed". High-expectation customer. Ignore people who would not miss it. |
| NEUMANN | Jerry Neumann, "Schumpeter on Strategy" — https://reactionwheel.net/2019/01/schumpeter-on-strategy.html | Profit is competed away. Excess profit needs cheaper inputs or better outputs, and something that slows imitation. |
